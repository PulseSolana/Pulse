# Pulse Score Components

Pulse is meant to highlight short-horizon pressure, not long-form narrative. The score should stay tied to live tape behavior.

## Inputs that matter most

- Aggressive buy or sell imbalance over a short rolling window.
- Breadth of unique participants joining the move.
- Liquidity migration across nearby levels after the first impulse.
- Whether follow-through appears after the initial burst instead of immediately fading.

## Downgrade signals when

- One wallet dominates the move.
- The tape spikes but the book refills instantly.
- Prints cluster in low-liquidity intervals that tend to overstate intent.

## Use

The score is best treated as an alerting layer for further review, not as a standalone trade trigger.
